## Executive directive

Keep the StatScout brand and current US title. Do not trade a proven branded and Statcast search cluster for a generic title. First repair the live screenshot inventory and replace unsupported or wrong-intent keywords. Then test the shorter subtitle separately, after a clean measurement window.

The highest-priority change is not the title. It is the live screenshot defect: ASC currently serves the leaderboard twice in positions 1 and 2, then the player profile twice in positions 3 and 4. Trends does not appear until position 5. Remove every duplicate and rebuild the first three as Leaderboard, Player Profile, Trends.

The second priority is keyword hygiene. The deployed field spends bytes on `wrc`, `wrcplus`, and `fantasy`. The current product does not expose wRC or wRC+, and it has no fantasy rankings, projections, draft tools, league management, or DFS workflow. Replace those terms with literal product concepts that can combine with `baseball`, `MLB`, `Statcast`, and `stats`.

## Measurement plan

| Layer | Metrics | Decision rule |
|---|---|---|
| Indexing | Rank and ranking history for exact and compound queries | Verify that retained high-value compounds remain stable and new player or leaderboard compounds enter the result set. |
| Search exposure | Search impressions by territory | A gain is useful only if page-view and download quality do not deteriorate. |
