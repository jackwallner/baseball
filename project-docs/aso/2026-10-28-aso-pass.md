# App Store ASO pass, 2026-10-28

Scope: StatScout metadata for en-US, en-CA, es-MX, ja, zh-Hant and ko, plus the extra US-indexed keyword fields ru, zh-Hans, ar-SA, fr-FR, pt-BR and vi. No app source, pricing, ad, review or release work was done.

The 90-day installs support this market order: US 674, Canada 34, Mexico 26, Japan 24, Taiwan 11 and South Korea 11. The United States remains the conversion focus. Canada retained its established English title and subtitle. Localized titles and subtitles changed only where the stored copy was awkward or truncated and local search evidence supported a clearer phrase.

The June 25 plan and August 27 audit decisions were retained: US title and subtitle stayed fixed, top-10 terms were protected, and unsupported `wrc`, `wrcplus` and `fantasy` terms were removed. The prior Mexico, Japan and Korea title/subtitle recommendations were applied. Taiwan had no earlier title recommendation; its localized title and subtitle were updated from local autocomplete and competitor language, with the rank risk called out below. No prior decision was overturned.

Apple's [App Store localization reference](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/app-store-localizations) lists the additional locales indexed in the US storefront. The App Store Search API returned usable ranks for some initial queries, then returned HTTP 403 on later calls. Ranks below are only successful live results captured on October 28. An absent rank means no result appeared within the returned result set, or the API blocked the query. Current SERPs for wrc, wrcplus and fantasy could not be refreshed; their removal follows the August audit decision that those terms were unsupported. Autocomplete is first-party MZSearchHints and is treated as query evidence, not volume.

- **zh-Hans:** Added `baseball`, `stats` and `player`; kept `统计`, xwoba, oaa and the advanced metric vocabulary. The description contains no explicit English-only claim, so it was not rewritten as part of this supplemental keyword pass.
- **ar-SA:** Replaced wrc, wrcplus and fantasy with baseball and stats; kept xwoba, oaa, the metric terms and إحصائيات. Description punctuation was normalized to remove em dashes.
- **fr-FR:** Replaced wrc, wrcplus and fantasy with baseball. Did not add savant because it repeats the localized name. Kept xwoba, oaa and the rest of the English query vocabulary. Description punctuation was normalized to remove em dashes.
- **pt-BR:** Replaced wrc and fantasy with baseball and stats; kept Portuguese baseball/statistics terms, savant, xwoba, oaa and relevant English terms. Description punctuation was normalized to remove em dashes.
- **vi:** Replaced `wrc` and `fantasy` with `baseball`; kept Vietnamese baseball/statistics terms, savant, xwoba, oaa, sprint, velo, strike and dfs. Description punctuation was normalized to remove em dashes.

No explicit English-only copy was found in the ko, zh-Hans or de-DE descriptions. The ko description is now Korean as part of its listed market work. zh-Hans and de-DE are in-app localization landing markets but were not full metadata markets in this pass.

**Risk:** These additional locales were adjusted as US keyword space, not as full local market rewrites. Their ranks in their own storefronts were not evaluated. The supplemental English terms can help US query matching, but do not establish demand in the corresponding local language.
