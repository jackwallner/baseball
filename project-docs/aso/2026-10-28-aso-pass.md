This review overturns that keyword-removal decision, because the live local search results show protected top-20 combinations and no stronger replacement evidence was established. Rankings alone do not establish that those searches are relevant or convert.

The US and Canadian promotions now say qualified players, not every player. All six priority-market descriptions explicitly say following players is free, and the English purchase summary states that both subscription options have a seven-day trial. Punctuation was repaired in other descriptions to satisfy the no-em-dash rule.

Greek duplicated keyword tokens were removed only where the identical words remain in its name or subtitle. They remain available to form queries. The available space was filled with baseball and analytics after an independent Greek search ranked baseball analytics #3.

The Japanese, Mexican, Korean and Traditional Chinese controls still have awkward or truncated title/subtitle copy. Native review remains outstanding for the kept localized descriptions. Current title and subtitle ranks are not claimed to have been comprehensively mapped.
