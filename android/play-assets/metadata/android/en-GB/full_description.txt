See the baseball story beyond the box score.

StatScout brings Statcast percentiles, standard season stats, and recent form into one fast baseball scouting app. Find league leaders, follow your players, and see what makes an elite hitter or pitcher.

VISUAL STATCAST PERCENTILES
Read xwOBA, xBA, exit velocity, barrel rate, hard-hit rate, strikeout rate, and more as clear percentile bars. Red marks the high end and blue the low end. Higher percentiles mean better performance.

COMPLETE PLAYER PROFILES
Explore hitting, pitching, fielding, and running. See the standard line alongside advanced metrics, with baseball's familiar number formatting and separate stats for two-way players.

CURRENT-SEASON LEADERS, FREE
Search players and teams, change the metric, adjust the qualifier, and build your Following list. Open a player profile in one tap. Current-season leaders, team season scouting, and following are free.

POSTSEASON IN CONTEXT
Regular-season and postseason data stay separate. October player values are placed on the same year's regular-season percentile curve, with clear labels and MLB-style playing-time qualification.

STATSCOUT+
Unlock the full scouting toolkit:
- Trends: see who is heating up and cooling off across the league
- Recent Form: last 7, 15, or 30 calendar days, with change from the prior window
- Player Comparisons: two players side by side across every available metric
- Year-over-Year: compare a player's seasons back to 2015
- Team Recent Form: last 7, 15, or 30 days for all 30 clubs
- Historical Seasons: explore the full Statcast era

Choose Monthly, Yearly, or Lifetime. Plan prices and trial eligibility appear in the app before purchase.

UPDATED NIGHTLY
The baseball data pipeline refreshes nightly. Coverage dates show which games are included, so you can judge the numbers in context.

NO ACCOUNT REQUIRED
No sign-up and no ads. Followed players and preferences stay on your phone. Google Play handles payments.

Subscriptions
