How it's built.

The Pitch

Most baseball analytics tools are buried in cluttered websites or locked behind paywalls. I wanted the numbers readable on a phone.

StatScout reads nightly Statcast data, calculates percentile rankings for every qualified player, and surfaces it all in one scannable view. No ads, no tracking, no account. The core features are free.

What Mattered

Percentiles as the interface. Instead of tables of raw numbers, StatScout ranks every player on a 0–100 scale and shows it as a visual bar. One glance tells you if someone is elite (90+), average, or below.

Nightly automation. The data pipeline runs every night via GitHub Actions: fetch, compute percentiles, push to Supabase. By morning, the app has fresh rankings.

Spotlight metric prioritization. Each player profile highlights their top (and bottom) metrics so you can instantly see what a player does best and where they struggle.

Privacy. No accounts, no analytics, no crash reporting. StatScout doesn't know who you are. The app simply fetches public data and displays it.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about the app.

Is there a Baseball Savant app for iPhone?

Baseball Savant is MLB's website, and I found no official Savant app on the App Store. Baseball Savvy StatScout is an independent iPhone app that shows Savant-style Statcast percentile rankings, and it is not affiliated with MLB.

What is a baseball percentile rankings app?

It ranks each player against the rest of the league on a 0 to 100 scale for every metric. Baseball Savvy StatScout shows that as a color-coded bar, from blue at the low end through gray to red at the top.

What is xwOBA?

xwOBA (expected weighted on-base average) estimates how well a hitter should be doing based on exit velocity and launch angle rather than on the actual outcomes. StatScout shows it as a percentile next to metrics like Hard-Hit%, Barrel% and Sprint Speed.

How often is the data updated?

The data refreshes every night, so rankings reflect games through the previous day. You see leaderboards for hitting, pitching, and running.

Is Baseball Savvy StatScout free?

Yes, the core app is a free download with percentile rankings, leaderboards and team rosters. An optional StatScout+ upgrade adds year-over-year comparisons, and prices are shown in the app before you buy and vary by region.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up, no ads and no third-party tracking.
