## Frequently Asked Questions

### How often is data updated?

StatScout data feeds refresh nightly after games have concluded. This ensures you have up-to-date percentile rankings and season stats every morning.

### Where does the data come from?

Our percentile rankings are calculated using public pitch-tracking data. We aggregate this data to provide a visual, easy-to-read comparison of every player in the league.

### Is my data private?

Yes. StatScout does not collect any personal information, search history, or location data. Your app preferences are stored locally on your device. [Read our Privacy Policy →](https://jackwallner.com/ios/baseball/privacy-policy.html)

### What devices are supported?

StatScout is built for iPhone and requires iOS 17 or later.

### Do I need an account?

No. StatScout works right out of the box with no sign-up or login required. Optional Pro purchases unlock historical and comparison features.
