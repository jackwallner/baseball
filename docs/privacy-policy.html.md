# StatScout Privacy Policy

> Privacy policy for the Baseball Savvy StatScout iPhone app by Jack Wallner.

Web page: https://jackwallner.com/ios/baseball/privacy-policy.html

## StatScout Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

StatScout is a read-only baseball statistics app. It does not require an
account.

## Data used by the app

StatScout fetches publicly available baseball statistics from a
Supabase-hosted database. Network requests necessarily include technical
information such as an IP address, request time, user agent, and
response status. We use that information for delivery, security, and
reliability. We do not use it to build a profile or track you across
apps.

Your filters, selected categories, and other app preferences are stored
locally on your device. StatScout does not collect your name, email
address, contacts, photos, location, health data, search history, or
player-view history. It does not use advertising or analytics SDKs.

## Purchases and subscriptions

StatScout Pro includes monthly and yearly auto-renewing subscriptions
and a one-time lifetime purchase. Monthly and yearly plans may include a
7-day introductory free trial for eligible accounts, as shown by Apple
at purchase. Apple processes payment. RevenueCat verifies purchases and
entitlement status and may process an anonymous app user ID, transaction
and entitlement information, and limited technical app or device
context. It does not receive your baseball activity or personal contact
information. See
[RevenueCat's privacy policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/).

## Sharing and retention

We do not sell personal information or share it for advertising. Local
preferences remain until you delete them or remove the app. Supabase and
Apple may retain technical and transaction records under their own
policies. Contact us at the address below for a privacy request.

## Children

StatScout is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly
collect personal information from children.

## Changes

We may update this policy when the app or its services change. The date
above identifies the current version.
