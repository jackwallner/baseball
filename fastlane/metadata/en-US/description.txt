Baseball Savvy StatScout is an MLB advanced stats app for iPhone. See every qualified player's Statcast percentile rankings as color-coded bars, rank the league by xwOBA, Barrel%, sprint speed or Range (OAA), and follow your players. The current season is free, with no account and no ads.

VISUAL PERCENTILES
Every metric appears as a horizontal percentile bar with a colored marker. The color runs from blue at the 0th percentile through gray at the 50th to red at the 100th, so the league's best and worst results stand out at a glance.

HITTING, PITCHING, FIELDING AND RUNNING
Statcast metrics are grouped into four categories: expected stats such as xwOBA, xBA, xSLG and xERA, plus Barrel%, Hard-Hit%, Whiff%, Chase%, bat speed, fastball velocity and spin, Range (OAA), arm strength, sprint speed and more.

PLAYER PROFILES
Open any player for percentile rankings across the categories that apply to that player. Switch between Advanced and Standard stats, and share a percentile card as an image.

LEADERBOARDS
Rank the league by any Statcast metric or by traditional stats such as AVG, HR, RBI, OPS, ERA, WHIP and K/9. Filter by position or by qualifier level, and search for any player by name.

TEAMS
All 30 clubs, grouped by division. Each team page has Advanced, Standard and Roster views, with hitters and pitchers ranked by the stat you pick. Choose a favorite team to open it first.

POSTSEASON
Once the playoffs begin, switch the boards from the regular season to the postseason.

FOLLOWING
Follow players and a favorite team for free.

NIGHTLY UPDATES
During the season, the numbers refresh every night through the previous day's games. The app shows the date the data is current through.

FREE AND STATSCOUT+
The current season's leaderboards, player profiles, team pages and Following are free and do not expire. StatScout+ is an optional upgrade, as a monthly or yearly subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase. Subscriptions include a free trial for eligible new subscribers, and the app shows its length before you start. StatScout+ adds:
• Trends: players heating up or cooling off over the last 7, 15 or 30 days, for hitters and pitchers
• Recent Form: the same 7, 15 and 30 day windows on player profiles and team rosters
• Compare: any two players, any two teams, or one player year over year
• Best & Worst: the league leader and trailer on every metric
• Past Seasons: every season back to 2015

Prices appear in the app before you buy and vary by region. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Manage or cancel in Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions.

PRIVACY
No account or sign-up. No ads and no cross-app tracking. The app makes only the network requests needed for baseball statistics and purchase verification.

Statistics come from public baseball data sources, including Statcast.
