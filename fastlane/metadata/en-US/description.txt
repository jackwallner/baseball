VISUAL PERCENTILES
Each metric appears as a horizontal percentile bar with a color marker. Its color moves from blue (0th percentile), through gray (50th), to red (100th), making strong results easy to spot.

PLAYER PROFILES
Open a player to see percentile rankings across Hitting, Pitching, Fielding and Running. Switch between advanced metrics and standard season stats. Review performance trends with nightly-updated data.
