StatScout is a read-only baseball statistics viewer with no user accounts or sign-in.

Core flow:
1. Launch app. New users see onboarding; returning users land on the Stats board
2. Scroll the leaderboard of ~800+ players with percentile rankings
3. Tap a player for a profile with visual metric bars, standard stats, and year compare
4. Filter by Hitting, Pitching, Fielding, Running categories
5. Trends tab ranks the league by recent form; Teams tab browses rosters;
   Compare stacks two players head-to-head
6. The Stats tab's View menu holds Leaders and Best & Worst per metric

New in this version: Settings has a Language row that opens the app's page in iOS Settings, where the app's language can be changed. The rest is fixes.

StatScout+ features (IAP via RevenueCat):
- Historical seasons, player comparisons, year-over-year tracking
- Test with Sandbox account via RevenueCat

Postseason data: any board can be switched
between "Regular Season" and "Postseason" from the season menu on the
Stats and team screens. Before the first playoff game is ingested,
the menu keeps a visible but disabled "Postseason (after first playoff
game)" item so the feature is discoverable. Once playoff data lands,
it becomes selectable and the rows appear.

A postseason card on the Stats board. It is merchandising
only and grants nothing. For a free user its button opens the same
StatScout+ pitch reachable from the existing upgrade entry points; for
a subscriber it switches the boards to the postseason. There is no
promo code, no discount, and no price quoted anywhere in the card: all
prices come from StoreKit.

3.1.2(c), carried forward from the 1.4.4 review: the total billed
amount is the most conspicuous pricing element in every purchase flow.
Each purchase button has the price directly above it, set larger and
heavier than any other pricing text on the screen, including the
free-trial wording on the button and the small print beneath it.
Onboarding shows "$5.99 / month" above its button; the trial sheet and
the plan sheet show the selected plan's price the same way. That
pricing slot is a fixed height, so it cannot move the purchase button
while StoreKit is still loading.

2.1(b), also carried forward: both auto-renewable subscriptions

Guideline 4, from the 1.5.0 review on iPad Air 11-inch (M4): fixed.
StatScout is an iPhone app, so on iPad it runs in the iPhone
compatibility window, which is 375x622pt, around 140pt shorter than
the iPhone the onboarding flow had been laid out for. The flow now
measures the height it is actually given and sizes itself to it, so
every heading, every line of body copy and all four bullets are
visible on each page at that window size, with nothing truncated,
clipped or overlapped. The paging dots moved out of the card and into
the header, where they can no longer sit on top of a bullet. The same
fix covers iPhone SE, which has the same short canvas.

Guideline 2.1(b), the purchase error from the 1.5.0 review: fixed.
Two things could put a red message under a purchase button. Dismissing
Apple's confirm sheet was reported as "Purchase cancelled", which
reads as a failure rather than a choice, and cancelling is now silent. A
genuine failure showed whatever message was last set by any store
operation, including a product-load failure from launch, so a purchase
could inherit an unrelated "couldn't load subscription options" line;
purchase failures now carry their own StoreKit-specific copy. The
offering also retries on a slow or VPN'd network, and a CTA tapped
before products finish loading now loads them and buys, instead of
falling through to the plan picker's error state.

Known: Light mode only. No shadows. No accounts. No social features.
