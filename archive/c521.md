# StatScout UX Improvement Notes — c521

> User-perspective review of the iOS app as of May 21, 2026. Every item is framed as **what someone does in the app**, **what they feel**, and **what should change**. No code edits — recommendations only.

---

## Who is using this app?

StatScout targets fans and media who want **Savant-style percentile rankings on mobile**: scan the league, drill into a player, check a team roster, or compare two players / seasons. The free tier is anchored on the **current season (2026)**; Pro unlocks history, comparisons, recent-form windows, and on-demand historical loads.

The core loop is: **open app → find a player or team → read percentiles → optionally go deeper (standard stats, year-over-year, head-to-head, recent form)**.

---

## Journey map (how people actually move)

```text
Launch → Onboarding (2 cards) → Stats tab (default)
              │
              ├─ Leaders: season + search + category + sort → tap player → Profile
              ├─ Box Score: traditional stat leaders
              └─ Best/Worst: metric extremes by category

Teams tab → list or auto-jump to favorite team → roster → player profile

Compare tab → pick two players OR one player for year-over-year (Pro)

Profile → Percentiles | Standard Stats | Year Compare
              ├─ Recent Form card (Pro)
              ├─ Pro upsell card (free)
              └─ Compare toolbar (Pro)
```

## 1. First launch and onboarding

### What the user does
Skips or swipes through two cards (“Your Pocket Scout”, “Find Insights Fast”), taps **Get Started**, and lands on the Stats tab while data may still be loading.

### What they experience
- Onboarding copy still describes **four separate areas** (Leaders, Teams, Metrics, Stats), but the app has **three tabs** with Stats folding Leaders / Box Score / Best-Worst into one segmented control. New users build the wrong mental model and hunt for a “Metrics” tab that does not exist.
- Visual tone is improved (Statcast bar backdrop) but still feels like generic product marketing rather than “this is Savant on your phone.”
- **Get Started** always works (good — no trap behind purchase). Restore Purchases is available on the last card (good for reinstalls).
- Pro is **not** pitched on onboarding anymore (intentional deferral). That reduces pressure but means highly motivated installers never see value props until later interruptions.

### What should change
| Priority | Change |
|----------|--------|
| High | Rewrite onboarding bullets to match **Stats (Leaders / Box Score / Best-Worst) · Teams · Compare** — one sentence per tab, not four legacy names. |
| Medium | Add a single “Try it” hint on card 2: *“Search any player from Leaders”* so the first action after onboarding is obvious. |
| Medium | Optional third card or inline note: *“2026 season is free; Pro adds past seasons and comparisons”* — sets expectations before the first TrialPitchSheet. |
| Low | Stronger Savant visual language (navy slab, sample percentile bars with real metric names) so the first screen telegraphs the product category. |

## 2. League leaderboard (Stats → Leaders)

### What the user does
Picks a season, searches, filters by Hitting/Pitching/etc., changes sort via **Filters**, taps the sort chip to flip direction, pulls to refresh, scrolls the list, opens a player.

### What they experience
- **Strengths:** Search works; pull-to-refresh; relative “Updated …” freshness strip; empty states for bad search / no category data; active sort chip (“Sorted by xwOBA ↓ Highest first”) is a real improvement over a hidden-only sort menu.
- **Friction — changing what you sort by:** The metric itself still lives inside the **Filters** menu. Many users will only tap the center chip (direction) and never discover they can sort by Sprint Speed, Barrel%, etc. Screen recordings explicitly called this out: *“I can sort, but also go up or down”* — direction yes, metric switch less obvious.
- **Friction — season menu:** Past seasons show a crown in the menu; tapping fires a paywall (gated by `PaywallGate`, max 2 per trigger per session). Users who tap 2025 expecting a preview may feel blocked without explanation of what Pro includes.
- **Friction — header layout shift:** When `loadingStatusBar` appears/disappears under the control bar, the header jumps. Feels like a glitch on first load or refresh.
- **Friction — About / Pro:** “About StatScout” and “Unlock Pro” live only at the **bottom of a long scroll**. There is no Settings tab; users looking for subscription management or restore must know to scroll the leaderboard or use the nav-bar crown.
- **Data trust:** If nightly refresh is stale, “Updated yesterday at 6:04 AM” undermines confidence. Users cannot tell cache vs live without an error.

### What should change
| Priority | Change |
|----------|--------|
| High | Make **sort metric** discoverable on the same row as the sort chip — e.g. tappable metric name opens the picker, chevron on “Filters” only for qualifier. |
| Medium | When a locked season is tapped, show a one-line inline reason before paywall: *“2025 requires Pro — current season is free.”* |
| Medium | Stabilize header height (reserve space for loading strip) so the bar does not jump. |
| Medium | Persistent **cached data** banner when showing offline/stale cache: *“Showing saved data from [date] — pull to refresh.”* |
| Low | Surface **About** from nav bar (info icon) so restore, legal, and last-updated are not buried. |

## 3. Stats → Box Score and Best/Worst

### What the user does
Switches segmented control to **Box Score** (traditional stats) or **Best/Worst** (league extremes per metric).

### What they experience
- No Pro upsell banners here (good — consistent with actual access).
- **Box Score** and **Best/Worst** purpose is unclear without exploration. “Stats” sounds like one thing; users may not realize Best/Worst answers *“who’s best and worst at each Statcast metric?”*
- Best/Worst has **no search or jump-to-metric** — long scroll through categories.
- Tapping a leader opens player profile (good), but there is no path back that preserves which sub-mode you were in (minor).

### What should change
| Priority | Change |
|----------|--------|
| Medium | One-line subtitles under segment labels: *“Traditional counting stats”* / *“Top & bottom per metric”*. |
| Medium | Search or sticky category index on Best/Worst for users looking for one stat (e.g. xwOBA). |
| Low | Show player counts per category on the category filter (e.g. Fielding (12)) to avoid empty taps. |

## 4. Player profile (highest-traffic screen)

### What the user does
Arrives from leaderboard, team roster, or search; lands on **Percentiles** by default; may switch to Standard Stats or Year Compare; scrolls Recent Form; hits compare in toolbar; changes season in the percentile header.

### What they experience

**Improvements since earlier audits (worth keeping):**
- Free users see **full percentile lists** for the current season — not a blank paywall wall.
- **Year Compare** tab switches for everyone; free users see `YearComparePreview` (blurred mock + CTA) instead of an instant paywall on the tab button.
- **Standard Stats** respects the same season selection as percentiles (`displayedPlayer`).
- First visit shows **TrialPitchSheet** (half sheet, “Maybe later”) capped by `PaywallGate` — softer than a full-screen activation paywall.

**Remaining friction:**

1. **First meaningful tap still sells.** Opening any player triggers a trial pitch (session-capped). Users who just want to verify a stat from a group chat hit a subscription story before they’ve scrolled one metric row.

2. **Default tab is still “Percentiles.”** For users who care about AVG/HR/ERA, **Standard Stats** is one extra tap every time. Fantasy players often want counting stats first.

3. **Recent Form (Pro) sits above the fold but blurred.** Free users see a realistic blurred preview (good tease) but the card still **loads game logs** in the background (`task` on appear) — wasted work and battery for non-paying users.

4. **Pro upsell card at bottom of Percentiles.** After legitimate free content, a large “StatScout Pro” card repeats perks already shown in TrialPitchSheet. Scrolling a profile feels like a funnel: data → blur → sales.

5. **No swipe between tabs.** iOS users expect horizontal swipe among Percentiles / Standard / Year Compare; only pill buttons work.

6. **Metric rows are NavigationLinks.** Scrolling the percentile list can accidentally drill into `MetricRankingView` when the user meant to scroll — surprising detour.

7. **Season picker on profile vs league season.** Profile has its own season menu; league dashboard has another. Changing 2026 on the leaderboard does not automatically carry into an already-open profile (user may see two different years without understanding why).

8. **`previewMetrics` exists in code but is unused.** A “top 3 metrics” teaser + “See all →” pattern was started and abandoned — missed chance for clearer hierarchy on small screens.

9. **Compare toolbar (person.2).** Free tap → paywall, not picker. User cannot even **see** the comparison UI layout without paying (Compare tab is fully blurred too).

### What should change
| Priority | Change |
|----------|--------|
| High | Defer TrialPitchSheet until **second** profile open or until user taps a Pro-only control (Recent Form, past season, Compare). |
| High | Skip game-log fetch for Recent Form when `!isPro` — show static teaser only. |
| Medium | Remember last-used profile tab per user (or default Standard Stats for users who open it once). |
| Medium | Enable **TabView `.page` style** swipe between profile tabs. |
| Medium | Make metric rows non-navigable by default; add explicit “View league ranking →” affordance. |
| Medium | Sync or clearly label season scope: *“Showing 2026 — change season here”* vs league picker. |
| Low | Collapse or collapse-by-default Pro upsell card after first dismiss. |
| Low | Use top-3 metric summary at top of percentiles for faster scanning on phone. |

## 5. Year Compare (Pro) and preview (free)

### What the user does (free)
Taps Year Compare → sees blurred mock comparison → taps CTA → paywall.

### What the user does (Pro)
May need to tap **Load History** first → waits on progress → picks two years → reads dense grid of deltas.

### What they experience
- Preview communicates the **shape** of the feature (good).
- Real `YearComparisonView` is still **text-heavy and hard to scan** — screen review: *“looks a bit silly”*, wants clearer delta visualization (paired bars, stronger overall change).
- Pro users hit an extra **Load past seasons** step before history exists — correct for bandwidth, but feels like a chore if they only wanted one player’s arc.
- If history is thin or metrics align oddly, users may see confusing “no change” rows — erodes trust in the comparison engine.

### What should change
| Priority | Change |
|----------|--------|
| High | Redesign year-over-year rows: side-by-side percentile bars + delta badge (reuse Recent Form paired-bar language). |
| Medium | Prefetch history when user opens Year Compare tab (with progress), not only via separate “Load History” card. |
| Medium | Free preview: use **this player’s** real overall percentile delta when available instead of generic mock numbers. |
| Low | Empty state explains *why* years are missing (rookies, insufficient sample, not loaded yet). |

## 6. Recent Form and Team Form (Pro gates)

### What the user does
On a player profile, toggles **7 / 15 / 30** day windows. On a team page, sees team-level recent form.

### What they experience
- Window picker is clear; **SMALL SAMPLE** badge sets expectations (good).
- Free: blurred content + “Unlock with Pro” — honest tease.
- Pro: paired season vs recent bars are strong for “is he heating up?” — aligns with how fans actually argue about players.
- **Gap:** No explanation on first Pro use of what “percentile for last 15 days” means vs full season — one line in the card header would help non-experts.

### What should change
| Priority | Change |
|----------|--------|
| Medium | One-tap **“What is Recent Form?”** link beside RECENT FORM header (reuse percentile info pattern). |
| Low | For Pro, default window to **15 days** (already default) but remember last choice per user. |

## 7. Teams tab

### What the user does
Opens Teams → may be **auto-navigated** into favorite team on first visit → browses roster → filters Hitting/Pitching → opens players.

### What they experience
- Favorite team auto-enter is helpful for repeat fans but **surprising on first visit** — video review: *“bugs out”* when the list flashes away into a team.
- Alphabetical list (no fake “team score”) is clearer than old ordering.
- Team **Recent Form** mirrors player card gating.
- Past seasons in team season menu trigger paywall (consistent with league).
- Roster sort header toggles direction; category tabs work like leaderboard.

### What should change
| Priority | Change |
|----------|--------|
| High | First Teams visit: show list with banner *“Jump to [Favorite]”* instead of silent `NavigationPath` push — user chooses entry. |
| Medium | Loading skeleton for team list when `teamsWithData` is empty on first paint. |
| Low | Long-press team to set favorite without opening team page. |

## 8. Compare tab

### What the user does
Opens dedicated Compare tab to set Player A vs B or pick one player for year-over-year.

### What they experience
- Entire surface is **blurred and disabled** for free users with overlay *“Compare is a Pro feature.”* They cannot interact with pickers to understand the flow.
- Pro users get a clean hub; historical load kicks off on appear (good).
- Duplicates entry points already on profile (toolbar compare, Year Compare tab) — three doors, same lock.

### What should change
| Priority | Change |
|----------|--------|
| High | Let free users **use the pickers and see empty slots**; gate only the final **Compare** navigation (mirror Year Compare preview pattern). |
| Medium | Single in-app sentence on Compare tab: *“Same as Compare button on a player page.”* |
| Low | After Pro purchase, auto-focus Compare tab once with checklist overlay. |

## 9. Subscription, paywall, and trust

### What the user does
Hits crown in nav bar, footer Unlock, season lock, Recent Form, Compare, TrialPitchSheet → RevenueCat paywall → purchase or dismiss.

### What they experience
- **PaywallGate** (max 2 per trigger per session) reduces nag fatigue — good.
- Contextual headers per trigger (`PaywallView.triggerHeader`) help explain *why* this sheet appeared.
- Toolbar **Try Pro / Get Pro** pill is clearer than a bare “Pro” badge.
- **Gaps:**
  - Purchase success: sheet dismisses with **no confirmation** — user unsure Pro unlocked.
  - Paywall copy vs reality: marketing still mentions perks that match current gates, but **Recent Form** and **full team scouting** are the emotional sell while **current-season percentiles are already free** — value story takes work to understand.
  - Pricing selection issues were reported in review (wrong amounts, selection not moving) — if still present in RevenueCat UI, directly blocks conversion.
  - **Restore** exists in About and paywall footer (good); About is hard to find from Compare/Teams without scrolling leaderboard footer.
  - No **Manage Subscription** deep link, renewal date, or plan name for active subscribers in About.

### What should change
| Priority | Change |
|----------|--------|
| High | Post-purchase moment: checkmark + *“Pro unlocked”* + 3-bullet “now you can …” + link to Manage Subscription. |
| High | Verify RevenueCat offering prices and selection state in TestFlight before next release. |
| Medium | Consolidate passive upsells: pick **one** of {nav crown, leaderboard footer, profile bottom card} for a given session. |
| Medium | About → add Manage Subscription (`apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions`), plan type, renewal for Pro. |
| Low | Show savings math on yearly tier (*“$4.99/yr ≈ $0.42/mo”*). |

## 10. Cross-cutting gaps (every journey)

### Favorites and return visits
- **Team favorite** exists; **player favorites** do not. Fantasy users re-search the same 10–15 names every session.

**Change:** Player watchlist on Stats tab (pinned section or filter chip “★ Watchlist”).

### Sharing
- `Player.shareSummary` is implemented; **no Share button** in UI.

**Change:** Share icon on profile header → `ShareLink` / activity sheet for group chats and Twitter.

### Appearance
- App forces **light mode** (`.preferredColorScheme(.light)`). Night-game viewers get blasted.

**Change:** Respect system appearance or offer Savant dark canvas variant.

### Education
- Percentile ⓘ sheet exists on profile (good).
- Many metrics (xwOBA, Barrel%, etc.) have no plain-language gloss outside that sheet.

**Change:** Tap metric label → one-sentence explainer sheet (could be shared with Best/Worst rows).

### Post-Pro discovery
- After upgrade, UI mostly removes locks — no guided *“try Year Compare on Judge”* moment.

**Change:** One-time coach marks on Compare tab and profile Year Compare after first Pro unlock.

### Accessibility
- Good use of accessibility labels on filters/season; metric bars and color-only percentile encoding may be hard for color-blind users.

**Change:** Ensure percentile tier is duplicated as text on bars, not only hue.

## 12. What not to regress

These are recent UX wins — any future change should preserve them:

- Current-season **full percentiles** for free users on player profiles.
- **Year Compare** tab shows preview content before paywall, not a tab-button trap.
- **TrialPitchSheet** + `PaywallGate` instead of endless full-screen activation paywalls.
- **activeSortChip** on leaderboard for sort direction.
- **Standard Stats** tied to `displayedPlayer` season.
- Onboarding **Get Started** never blocked on purchase or network.
- **Restore Purchases** on onboarding last card and in About.
